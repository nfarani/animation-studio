# STYLE BIBLE — channel house style

Authoritative. Built from actual creator approvals and project experience, never generic buzzwords.
Each rule: the rule · source (project + date) · example shot/asset if any.
The first /make drafts initial rules marked PROVISIONAL; they become CONFIRMED only when the creator approves them.

> **Status of this file:** entries marked **CREATOR FEEDBACK** come from the creator's own notes. Every other rule below is **PROVISIONAL**, drafted during `/make` for `the-last-game` on 2026-10-06. None has been approved by the creator and none is backed by a generated image yet. Rules taken directly from the creator's script are marked *(from script)*; the rest are the director's proposals.

## CHARACTER DESIGN
(proportions, face philosophy, eye size, stylisation level, silhouette philosophy)

- PROVISIONAL · Stylised but grounded proportions: heads slightly large (about 1:5.5 for children, 1:7 for adults), hands and feet a little oversized. Not chibi, not realistic. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Medium-sized eyes. Expression comes from brows, eyelids and mouth corners, not from eye size. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Every recurring character has one or two features that survive in silhouette (Leo: ears + crown tuft; Sam: round curls + foot on the ball). · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · CHAR_LEO_BOY_MASTER, CHAR_SAM_MASTER
- PROVISIONAL · Faces are slightly asymmetric and imperfect: a tooth gap, uneven socks, a tuft that will not lie flat. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Clothing is plain. No logos, text, numbers or stripes. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06

## ENVIRONMENTS
(realism level, texture density, prop density, architecture behaviour)

- PROVISIONAL · Real-world places, simplified. Architecture is straight and believable, not wonky or whimsical. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Tactile, readable materials (rusted wire, worn grass, brick), with texture detail kept highest where the story looks. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Low prop density. If an object is in frame it is there for a reason. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · A recurring location has a fixed plan and named camera positions before any shot is designed. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · 06_environment_bible.md
- PROVISIONAL · When a location is shown in two time periods, the later plate is made by editing the earlier one so the frames align. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · ENV_PITCH_THEN_A → ENV_PITCH_NOW_A

## LIGHTING
(preferred contrast, warmth, time-of-day treatment)

- PROVISIONAL · Light is motivated by a real source and a real time of day. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Memory / childhood: low golden-hour sun, long shadows, rim light, dust in the air. *(from script: "Golden-hour evening")* · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Present-day reflection: soft overcast, low contrast, no cast shadows. Flat but not gloomy. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Light does not change within a shot to underline an emotion (no sunbreak on the sad beat). · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · S07_SH01

## COLOUR
(saturation, recurring palettes, emotional colour rules)

- PROVISIONAL · Saturation follows emotional temperature: the past is saturated and warm, the present is desaturated by about a third and cool. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Each main character owns a colour that persists across time (Leo: mustard as a boy, ochre as a man). · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · In a desaturated scene, allow exactly one warm accent, and put it on the protagonist. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · S05_SH01

## CAMERA
(lens feeling, movement philosophy, framing preferences)

- PROVISIONAL · Static camera is the default. Movement must be justified by momentum, discovery or comedy. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · 2 moving shots of 22
- PROVISIONAL · Camera height follows the character whose experience the shot belongs to (child eye level for the children). · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · A locked, repeated frame is a preferred device for showing time or change. *(from script: "Same exact angle")* · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · ANGLE A
- PROVISIONAL · The camera is allowed to stay behind when a character leaves. *(from script: "Camera stays behind")* · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · S07_SH01
- PROVISIONAL · Wide and natural lens feel (24–50 mm) for action; long lens (85 mm+) only for the few close-ups that carry emotion. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06

## ANIMATION
(movement exaggeration, physical weight, comedy timing, facial expressiveness)

- PROVISIONAL · Physical weight is believable: bags slump, balls sit heavy, bodies need a step to recover balance. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Children move with their whole body and overshoot; adults move less and later. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Adult facial performance is small: millimetres of brow, eyes before mouth. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · S05_SH02, S06_SH03
- PROVISIONAL · A character's habit (a gesture, a way of standing) is a continuity asset and is reused. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · thumb strap hitch

## EDITING
(average pacing, transition language)

- PROVISIONAL · Pace follows the emotion, not a fixed rate: fragments of 1–1.5 s for play, holds of 2 s and more for reflection. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Hard cuts. Transitions are motivated by a body, an object or a sound, never a dissolve by default. *(from script: body wipe; "Cut to black")* · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · S04_SH01
- PROVISIONAL · Memory is cut in hard, with sound leading by a few frames, and without a visual effect announcing it. *(from script: "FLASH")* · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · A flashback reuses the original footage exactly. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · S05_SH03
- **CREATOR FEEDBACK** · Do not repeat the same shot or object: a recurring insert (the backpacks appeared four times) read as "repetitive and slow". One appearance per idea unless the repeat is the point. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06, creator on rough cut v1
- **CREATOR FEEDBACK** · No voice may be cut off by an edit. Let a line run across the picture cut rather than trimming it. Check every cut against the spoken lines before delivering. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06, creator on rough cut v1
- **CREATOR FEEDBACK** · A cut delivered for review must already be clean enough to publish: no reversed clips, no frozen stills, no abrupt sound changes. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06, creator on rough cut v1 ("some of the editing is weird")

## COMEDY
(visual humour style, exaggeration level)

- PROVISIONAL · Comedy is behavioural and physical, at real-world scale: a ball poked away, a disputed goal. No cartoon exaggeration or sound effects. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · S01_SH03

## EMOTION
(preferred restraint, behaviours to favour, behaviours to avoid)

- PROVISIONAL · Restraint. The audience is given the facts and left to feel them. *(from script: "A small smile. Not sad.")* · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Favour: a pause, a hand resting on something and leaving it, a look at an empty place, walking on. *(from script)* · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · S06_SH04, S07_SH01
- PROVISIONAL · Avoid: tears, trembling, looking down sadly, a character explaining what they feel. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · No voiceover and no closing text when the images already say it. *(from script: "No voiceover. No closing text.")* — applies to this film; whether it is a channel rule is for the creator to say. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Sound may carry a memory by itself. Silence is allowed to end a film. *(from script)* · the-last-game, 2026-10-06 · S07_SH01

## AVOID
(visual or storytelling tendencies the creator dislikes)

- PROVISIONAL · On-screen text, captions, logos, signage. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Dereliction as shorthand for time passing (graffiti, litter, broken glass). Time is shown by growth and wear. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Score that tells the audience how to feel; music over a final beat. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- PROVISIONAL · Camera movement added for its own sake; slow push-ins on emotional faces. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
- (No creator dislikes recorded yet.)
- **CREATOR FEEDBACK** · Slow, repeated insert shots. · the-last-game, 2026-10-06
