# 06 — ENVIRONMENT BIBLE

One location in two eras. The geography is identical in both; only surface, growth and light change.

## THE PITCH — fixed geography (both eras)

```
                         NORTH
   ┌──────────────── back gardens / brick terrace ───────────────┐
   │  T      T      T      T      T      T      (6 trees)       │  north fence
   │                                                             │
   │                                                             │
 W │      [S]                                                    │ E
 E │       ↕  goal          ● Sam's spot (centre)                │ A
 S │      [L]                                                    │ S
 T │                                                             │ T
   │                                                             │
   └───────────── south fence ─────────────┐  GATEWAY  ┌─────────┘
   ═══════════════════ footpath ═══════════╧═══════════╧══════════ → Leo's home (east)
                                              ▲
                                           CAM A (on the verge south of the path, 4 m back, looking NW)
                         SOUTH
```
`[S]` Sam's navy backpack · `[L]` Leo's red backpack · `T` tree

- **Scale:** small, roughly 30 m × 18 m. A kickabout pitch, not a marked football field. No lines, no fixed goals, no floodlights.
- **Fence:** chain-link, ~3 m high, on galvanised steel posts, all four sides.
- **Gateway:** one opening in the south fence, toward the east end, ~1.5 m wide. **No gate leaf in either era.** It is always open.
- **Footpath:** tarmac path along the outside of the south fence, running east–west. Leo's home is east (frame right from CAM A).
- **North side:** six trees in a row just inside the north fence; behind the fence, the backs of a brick terrace (garden walls, upper windows, chimneys).
- **Backpack goal:** west half of the pitch, bags ~3 m apart on a north–south line, so the goalmouth faces east.
- **Sam's spot:** the centre of the pitch.
- **Sun:** sets in the west. In all THEN shots from the south side, light comes from frame left and shadows fall to the right.
- **Mother's voice:** comes from the south-east, beyond the gateway.

## ERA 1 — THEN
- **Time:** golden hour, early autumn. Sun 10–15° above the horizon in S1–S2, lower (5–8°) from S3.
- **Fence:** intact, taut, dull silver. Posts upright.
- **Surface:** short grass, green, with honest wear: a bare earth patch in the goalmouth and a faint worn line across the centre.
- **Trees:** **six young saplings, ~2 m tall, each tied to a wooden stake.** Thin, sparse leaves.
- **Terrace:** warm red brick, lit amber on the west-facing surfaces; a few windows catch the sun.
- **Lighting:** low, warm, directional from the west. Long shadows to the east. Rim light on hair and shoulders. Soft bounce from the grass.
- **Palette:** amber, warm green, brick red, long blue-violet shadows. Saturated but not candy.
- **Atmosphere:** light dust kicked up in the low sun; midges in the backlight. A lived-in place at its best hour.
- **Ambient sound:** children's voices, ball impacts, a dog barking somewhere, a quiet suburban evening. No music source.

## ERA 2 — NOW (≈ 22 years later)
- **Time:** late afternoon, overcast. No visible sun, no cast shadows.
- **Fence:** same fence. **Rust at every joint, the wire sagging between posts, one post left of the gateway leaning a few degrees.** No holes, no graffiti, no signs.
- **Surface:** grass grown long and uneven at the edges, **worn to bare earth across the middle**, dandelions and plantain coming through. No goalmouth patch distinguishable any more.
- **Trees:** **the same six, now 9–10 m tall with full crowns** that overhang the north fence and hide most of the terrace. The stakes are gone.
- **Terrace:** same brick, cooler and darker; mostly hidden by the trees.
- **Lighting:** soft, flat, cool. Low contrast. Sky a pale even grey.
- **Palette:** grey-green, slate, cool brown. Desaturated by about a third against THEN. **The only warm colour is Leo's ochre jumper.**
- **Atmosphere:** still, with wind moving the tree crowns and the long grass. Not derelict, not threatening. Simply not used.
- **Ambient sound:** wind in leaves, distant traffic, the fence wire ticking faintly against a post.

## What ages and what must not
| Element | THEN | NOW | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fence line, posts, gateway position | — | — | **Identical position in frame** |
| Tree positions (6) | saplings with stakes | mature | Same spacing, same count |
| Terrace roofline | visible | mostly hidden | Same where visible |
| Path edge, horizon line | — | — | Identical |
| Backpacks, ball, children | present | **absent** | Nothing left behind in NOW |
| Litter, graffiti, signage, text | none | none | Never |
