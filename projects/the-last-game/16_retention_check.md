# 16 — RETENTION CHECK

A 34-second film has no room for a slow patch. Checked beat by beat.

## First 5 seconds (0:00–0:04.8) — PASS
- Opens mid-action: a boy running, low sun, voices already going. No title, no establishing pause.
- By 0:03.3 the viewer has seen a satisfying, wordless idea: two backpacks make a goal.
- By 0:04.8 the ball has been stolen and everyone is running. Comedy and motion.
- **Weakness:** nothing in the first five seconds signals that this is more than a nice football clip. That is intended; the film's turn depends on the viewer not expecting it. The title ("The Last Game") does the foreshadowing.

## First 15 seconds — PASS
- 0:04.8–0:09.6: four fragments, each a different scale and angle. No two consecutive shots look alike.
- 0:09.6: the mother's voice changes the rhythm. The film slows, and for the first time holds on a face and lets a line land. A viewer feels the emphasis even before they know why.
- 0:15.1: the body wipe.

## The cut (0:15.1–0:18.5) — the film's hook, arriving at the midpoint
- Sound dropping to nothing, then wind, is as much of a hook as the image.
- Two seconds of an empty frame is long for a short. It is kept because the viewer needs time to compare the frame with the one they just saw: fence, trees, no bag. **Do not shorten below 1.7 s.**
- Audience question created: what happened?

## Ending — PASS
- Ends on an image (the empty pitch) and a sound (silence), not an explanation.
- The last frame is a changed version of an earlier frame, three times over.
- The half-second pause is the only "hook" in the ending and it is in the script.
- No text, per the creator. A viewer who wants a caption will not get one; that restraint is the film's character.

## Rewatch value
High for its length. On a second viewing "Yeah, obviously", the bag left alone, and the ball left with Sam all read differently. Short runtime plus a reason to rewatch is good for loop-based platforms.

## Risks to retention
1. **The childhood half reads as generic.** If Scenes 1–2 look like stock "kids playing football", viewers leave before the cut. Mitigation: specific character design, the backpack goal as a real idea in shot 2, and fast varied coverage.
2. **The adult is not recognised as the boy.** Then the second half is a stranger looking at a field. Mitigation: `13_production_risk.md` item 2.
3. **The 34-second length on a platform that favours ≤ 30 s.** Mitigation: the 30 s trim.

## Not added
No on-screen text, no opening question, no countdown, no music sting at the cut. Any of them would cheapen it.
