# 03 — REFINED WORKING SCRIPT

Working version built from `00_source/idea_and_script.md`. Story content unchanged. Changes are staging and one cut line; each is listed in `02_creative_notes.md`. Character names are working names for production and are never spoken.

Dialogue tags: **(ON)** on-screen lip-sync · **(O.S.)** off-screen · **(NLS)** on-screen with no lip-sync pass (turned away, or small in frame and moving).

---

## SCENE 1 — ARRIVAL · 0:00.0–0:04.8 · THEN, golden hour

LEO (12) runs in through the gap in the fence, ball under his arm. SAM, DANNY and KIT are already on the pitch. Sam's navy backpack is on the grass.

Ground level: Leo's red backpack lands in the foreground, opposite Sam's. Goalposts.

The ball is at Leo's feet for less than a second. A foot pokes it away.

**LEO (O.S.)** Oi!

Laughter. Feet run after it.

## SCENE 2 — THE GAME · 0:04.8–0:09.6 · THEN

Fragments.

Leo's feet chasing the ball over worn grass.

A shoulder comes in from the side. Leo stumbles, nearly goes down, keeps running.

Behind the goal, ground level: the ball rolls between the two backpacks.

**SAM (O.S.)** That wasn't in!
**LEO (O.S.)** Course it was!

Laughter. Danny scoops the ball up in his hands and runs off with it. Leo goes after him.

## SCENE 3 — CALLED HOME · 0:09.6–0:15.1 · THEN, sun lower

**MOTHER (O.S.)** Come on! Dinner!

Leo, mid-run, stops and turns his head toward the voice.

**LEO (NLS)** Coming!

His hand takes the red backpack off the grass. Sam's navy one is left there by itself.

Sam stands beside the ball, one foot resting on it.

**SAM (ON)** You coming tomorrow?

Leo is already backing away, hitching the strap up his shoulder with his thumb.

**LEO (NLS)** Yeah, obviously.

He turns and runs.

## SCENE 4 — THE CUT · 0:15.1–0:18.5

From outside the gateway: Leo runs straight at us and passes very close. His mustard shirt fills the frame.

When it clears —

Same angle. Same pitch. Years later. Empty.

The fence leans and has rusted. The grass is worn to earth in places and weeds have come up. The saplings along the far fence are full-grown trees.

Distant traffic. Wind.

## SCENE 5 — RECOGNITION · 0:18.5–0:25.3 · NOW

Same frame. A man in his thirties walks in along the path, hitching a bag strap up his shoulder with his thumb. LEO. He is going somewhere. He slows. Stops at the fence. Looks in.

Close, through the wire: his face. Something shifts. He has not thought about this place in a long time.

FLASH — Sam beside the ball, foot on it.

**SAM (ON)** You coming tomorrow?

The same view, now. No Sam. No ball. Grass.

## SCENE 6 — THE SECOND MEMORY · 0:25.3–0:29.7

Low, golden: boys run past, close. Someone laughing.

The ball bounces between the two backpacks.

Leo, now. A small smile. Not sad.

His hand rests on the wire. A second. He takes it away.

## SCENE 7 — WALKING ON · 0:29.7–0:34.0 · NOW

The first angle again. Leo turns from the fence and walks on. The camera stays.

Just before he leaves frame, very faintly:

**CHILD'S VOICE (O.S., memory)** Come on!

A ball is kicked. Children laugh.

He pauses. Half a second. He does not look back.

He walks out of frame.

The pitch is empty.

CUT TO BLACK.

Silence.
