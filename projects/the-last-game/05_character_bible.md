# 05 — CHARACTER BIBLE

Names are working names. None is spoken in the film. All clothing is plain: no logos, no text, no numbers, no stripes (stripes drift between generations).

---

## LEO — BOY
- **Role:** protagonist, THEN
- **Age:** 12
- **Height / build:** ~150 cm, slight and wiry, long forearms and shins, knees slightly too big for his legs
- **Face shape:** oval with a wide jaw; the adult face is already visible in it
- **Hair:** dark brown, short, with a **tuft at the crown that will not lie flat**
- **Eyes:** hazel, medium size, slightly downturned outer corners
- **Skin:** light olive, freckles across the nose only
- **Distinctive features:** **ears that stick out**; thick straight eyebrows
- **Clothing:** plain **mustard-yellow T-shirt**, dark teal shorts
- **Shoes:** scuffed white trainers with grey toe caps; **left sock pushed down, right sock up**
- **Accessories:** red backpack (`PROP_BACKPACK_LEO`), carried on the right shoulder only
- **Body language:** leans forward when he runs, as if falling into each step; never fully still
- **Typical expressions:** open-mouthed grin, mock outrage, eyes on the ball
- **Personality shown visually:** certain of everything; answers before the question has finished
- **Colour palette:** mustard, teal, red accent
- **Silhouette:** ears + crown tuft + one-shoulder backpack
- **Animation behaviour:** changes direction with his whole body at once. **Hitches the backpack strap up with his right thumb** when he starts moving.

## LEO — ADULT
- **Role:** protagonist, NOW. Must read as the same person without any text.
- **Age:** mid-thirties
- **Height / build:** ~180 cm, lean; the wiry boy grown up, shoulders slightly rounded from desk work
- **Face shape:** same oval and wide jaw, longer
- **Hair:** dark brown, shorter at the sides; **the crown tuft is still there**, smaller
- **Eyes:** same hazel, same downturned corners; faint lines beside them
- **Skin:** light olive; freckles faded but present; light even stubble
- **Distinctive features:** **same ears**, same straight eyebrows
- **Clothing:** charcoal wool overcoat worn open, **ochre knit jumper** (the mustard, aged), dark grey trousers
- **Shoes:** brown leather, clean
- **Accessories:** grey canvas messenger bag, strap on the **right shoulder**. No phone in hand, no headphones, no wedding-ring close-up, no glasses.
- **Body language:** walks upright and economically, eyes ahead, someone with somewhere to be. Slows by degrees rather than stopping at once.
- **Typical expressions:** neutral and inward; the smile, when it comes, starts at the eyes and barely reaches the mouth
- **Personality shown visually:** composed, a little tired, not unhappy
- **Colour palette:** charcoal, ochre, grey. The ochre is the only warm colour in the present-day frames.
- **Silhouette:** long coat + one-shoulder bag + the same ears
- **Animation behaviour:** small, late reactions. **Hitches the bag strap with his right thumb**, the same gesture as the boy. His hands otherwise stay in his coat pockets until `S06_SH04`.

**Continuity rule:** LEO ADULT is derived from LEO BOY. `CHAR_LEO_ADULT_MASTER` is generated using `CHAR_LEO_BOY_MASTER` as reference and is not approved unless the two read as one person side by side.

## SAM
- **Role:** Leo's closest friend. The face the audience must remember. THEN and memory only.
- **Age:** 12
- **Height / build:** ~146 cm, a little shorter than Leo and stockier
- **Face shape:** round
- **Hair:** tight black curls, short
- **Eyes:** dark brown, large, steady
- **Skin:** dark brown
- **Distinctive features:** **gap between the front teeth**, visible when he grins
- **Clothing:** plain faded **sky-blue long-sleeved top, sleeves pushed up to the elbows**; grey tracksuit bottoms
- **Shoes:** black trainers
- **Accessories:** navy backpack (`PROP_BACKPACK_SAM`) — on the grass as a goalpost for the whole film
- **Body language:** the still one. **When he stops, he puts one foot on top of the ball.** Hands on hips or hanging loose.
- **Typical expressions:** easy half-grin; asks questions with his eyebrows up
- **Personality shown visually:** unhurried; the one who stays last
- **Colour palette:** sky blue, grey, navy accent
- **Silhouette:** round head of curls + pushed-up sleeves + foot on ball
- **Animation behaviour:** moves less than everyone else. In `S03_SH03` he does not step, wave or follow; he asks and waits.

## DANNY
- **Role:** friend; scoops the ball up and runs (S02_SH04); his shoulder is the barge (S02_SH02)
- **Age / build:** 13, tallest of the four (~158 cm), lanky
- **Hair / skin:** short ginger hair, pale freckled skin
- **Clothing:** plain forest-green T-shirt, black shorts, grey trainers
- **Silhouette / behaviour:** all elbows; runs with the ball held high against his chest, laughing over his shoulder

## KIT
- **Role:** friend; the foot that pokes the ball away (S01_SH03)
- **Age / build:** 11, smallest (~138 cm)
- **Hair / skin:** cropped blond hair, fair skin, sunburnt nose
- **Clothing:** plain white T-shirt a size too big, navy shorts, red trainers
- **Silhouette / behaviour:** T-shirt flaps when he runs; always arrives a beat after the others

Danny and Kit are never held in close-up. They share one reference sheet (`CHAR_FRIENDS_SHEET`).

## MOTHER
Off-screen voice only. Never shown. Warm, slightly impatient, has shouted this a hundred times.
