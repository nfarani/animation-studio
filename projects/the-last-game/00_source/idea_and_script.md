Golden-hour evening.

A 12-year-old boy runs onto a small neighbourhood football pitch with the ball under his arm.

The boy drops his opposite it.

Goalposts.

Someone immediately kicks the ball away from him.

**BOY**  
Oi!

They laugh and chase after it.

Fast fragments of the game.

His feet chasing the ball.

A friend shoulder-barges him.

**FRIEND**  
That wasn't in!

**BOY**  
Course it was!

**FRIEND**  
You're joking!

They laugh.

Another friend grabs the ball and runs off with it.

The boy chases him.

From somewhere outside the pitch:

**MOTHER (O.S.)**  
Come on! Dinner!

**BOY**  
Coming!

He grabs his backpack.

His closest friend stays beside the ball.

**FRIEND**  
You coming tomorrow?

The boy is already backing away.

**BOY**  
Yeah, obviously.

He turns and runs home.

### 0:14–0:17

Same pitch.

Years later.

Empty.

The trees are bigger.

No children.

Just distant traffic and wind.

Same boy.

Looks in.

His friend standing beside the ball.

**FRIEND**  
You coming tomorrow?

Back to the adult.

Not sad.
