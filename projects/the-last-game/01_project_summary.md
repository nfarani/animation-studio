## Logline
A boy leaves a kickabout for dinner, promising to be back tomorrow. One cut later he is in his thirties, standing outside the same empty pitch, and realises that was the last time.

## What the film is about
Nobody knows which game is the last one. The film never says this. It shows a throwaway "Yeah, obviously" and then shows what came after.

## Intended feeling
Warm, then quiet. Recognition rather than grief. The script is explicit: the adult's smile is "not sad". The ending is a pause and a continued walk, not a breakdown.

## Emotional arc (protagonist)
careless joy → casual certainty ("obviously") → [time cut] → preoccupied → recognition → fondness → lets it go, keeps walking

## Key production idea
The film depends on one locked camera position (ANGLE A) used three times: the boy leaving, the reveal of the empty pitch, and the final shot. If ANGLE A THEN and ANGLE A NOW do not align, the film does not work. Everything in the asset plan is ordered to protect that.
