# 07 — PROP BIBLE

Four continuity-critical props. All are plain: no logos, no text, no brand shapes.

## PROP_BALL — the football
- **Appearance:** classic stitched-panel leather ball, **off-white with faded dark-blue panels** (not the black-and-white pattern; panel patterns mutate and the blue reads better in amber light)
- **Scale:** size 4; reaches just below a 12-year-old's knee
- **Material / wear:** scuffed leather, grey at the seams, slightly under-inflated so it sits heavily and does not bounce high
- **Owner:** Leo. He brings it (S01_SH01) and **leaves it behind with Sam** (S03_SH03). He never sees it again.
- **Begins:** under Leo's left arm
- **Moves:** Leo's arm → ground → kicked around → through the goal → Danny's hands → the ground at Sam's spot, under Sam's foot
- **Narrative importance:** high. It marks Sam's spot. Its absence in `S05_SH04` is the point of that shot.
- **NOW:** does not exist. There is no ball, no deflated remnant, anywhere in the present.

## PROP_BACKPACK_LEO — red backpack
- **Appearance:** plain school backpack, **faded red**, single front pocket, black zips, two black straps
- **Scale:** covers Leo's back from shoulders to waist
- **Material / wear:** canvas, softened, one corner scuffed pale; half-full, so it slumps when dropped
- **Owner:** Leo
- **Begins:** on Leo's right shoulder (S01_SH01)
- **Moves:** shoulder → dropped as the **south goalpost** (S01_SH02) → picked up (S03_SH02) → right shoulder (S03_SH04, S04_SH01)
- **Narrative importance:** high. One half of the goal; its removal breaks the goal.

## PROP_BACKPACK_SAM — navy backpack
- **Appearance:** plain school backpack, **navy blue**, same general shape as Leo's, grey zips
- **Material / wear:** canvas, newer than Leo's, fuller and more upright
- **Owner:** Sam
- **Begins:** already on the grass as the **north goalpost** when the film starts
- **Moves:** it does not. It stays in place through S1–S3 and both memories.
- **Narrative importance:** medium-high. The goalpost that is left standing alone.

## PROP_BAG_ADULT — grey messenger bag
- **Appearance:** plain grey canvas messenger bag, flap closed, dark webbing strap
- **Owner:** Leo, adult
- **Begins / stays:** right shoulder, bag at the left hip. Never put down, never opened.
- **Narrative importance:** low as an object, medium as an echo: one bag on the right shoulder, hitched with the right thumb, like the red backpack.

Phones, bicycles, water bottles, extra balls, fixed goalposts, benches, bins, signs, cars in frame. In NOW: nothing left behind by anyone.
