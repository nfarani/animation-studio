# 12 — CONTINUITY LEDGER

## Fixed for the whole film
- **Screen direction (south-side cameras):** west / goal / sun = frame left. East / gateway / home = frame right.
- **Leo carries on his right shoulder**, as a boy and as a man. He hitches with his right thumb.
- **Leo's left sock is down** in every THEN shot that shows it.
- **Ball:** off-white with faded dark-blue panels. One ball only.
- **Goal:** navy = north post, red = south post.
- **Trees:** six, same positions in both eras.
- **Gateway:** always open; no gate leaf.
- **No text, logos, numbers or signage anywhere.**
