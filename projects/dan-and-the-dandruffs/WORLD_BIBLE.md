## 1. VISUAL WORLD
Source: `00_source/visual_world_bible.md`

### CORE PRINCIPLE

Hair and scalp are the hidden origin of the world, not the visible aesthetic.

The dandruff civilisation exists because of Dan’s hair and scalp, but their world should NOT look like a literal medical close-up of human skin.

The world is fantastical, beautiful, lived-in, and imaginative.

Biology provides the underlying logic.

Fantasy and civilisation provide the visible experience.

### WORLD FEEL

The environment should feel:
- warm
- strange
- tactile
- imaginative
- lived-in
- slightly mysterious
- organic
- cinematic
- visually inviting

It should NOT feel:
- disgusting
- greasy
- medical
- dermatological
- infected
- dirty
- grotesque

### BIOLOGICAL INSPIRATION

Human hair becomes:
towering fibre-like natural structures

Scalp geography becomes:
soft ridges, valleys, layered terrain, cliffs and pathways

Hair roots/follicles inspire:
deep wells, foundations, caverns, structural anchors, neighbourhood centres

Sweat becomes:
mist, rain, dew, rising moisture, flooding

Scratching becomes:
massive moving structures entering the world and causing tremors

Washing becomes:
storms, floods and environmental upheaval

Anti-dandruff shampoo becomes:
the catastrophic Burning Tsunami

Lice become:
Long Foot creatures

### IMPORTANT

These transformations must remain visually poetic.

Do not show literal:
- pores
- greasy skin
- inflamed scalp
- flakes sitting on skin
- medical scalp photography
- dermatological textures

### HUMAN ELEMENTS

The civilisation should contain familiar social structures:
- homes
- schools
- shops
- laboratories
- public squares
- transport
- parks
- neighbourhoods
- institutions
- roads/pathways
- family life

These human elements should be adapted to the world rather than copied directly from modern Earth.

### ARCHITECTURE

Architecture should feel built from materials naturally available in the world.

Use:
- fibres
- layered sheet-like material
- thread-like structural elements
- pale organic surfaces
- translucent membranes
- natural rigid fragments
- handmade mechanical systems

Avoid:
- generic medieval fantasy
- mushroom houses
- steampunk cliché
- neon cyberpunk
- modern office buildings
- Pixar fantasy town

## 2. TERMINOLOGY
Source: `00_source/world_terminology.md`

### CORE PREMISE

Dan is an ordinary human boy.

Unknown to him, a civilisation of sentient dandruff beings exists within a fantastical micro-world connected to his hair and scalp.

The dandruffs do not understand the human world in the same way humans do.

They interpret external events using their own:
- science
- mythology
- terminology
- history
- cultural assumptions

The audience gradually learns what these terms correspond to.

Do not constantly explain the metaphors.

---

### THE BOY

Dandruff term:
The Boy

Human reality:
Dan

Dan is not worshipped as a god.

He is simply the enormous external being whose actions constantly affect their environment.

Most dandruffs have never directly seen him as a complete person.

---

### THE SACRED FOREST / MAJESTIC FOREST

Human origin:
Dan’s hair

Visual interpretation:
A vast world of towering fibre-like vertical structures.

IMPORTANT:
Do not depict literal strands of hair at normal human scale.

The biological origin should be understandable but visually transformed.

---

### FOREST TREES

Human origin:
Individual hairs

Visual interpretation:
Enormous dark fibre pillars or natural vertical structures.

They dominate the skyline.

---

### THE BURNING TSUNAMI

Human reality:
Anti-dandruff shampoo

Meaning:
The most catastrophic recurring event in dandruff history.

It arrives as an overwhelming blue-toned flood or storm-like liquid event.

It has caused enormous historical loss.

IMPORTANT:
“Burning” is experiential language.

Do NOT depict literal fire or lava.

---

### THE BIG WASH

Human reality:
Dan washing his hair normally

Meaning:
A dangerous but relatively common environmental event.

Effects may include:
- flooding
- violent currents
- shaking
- collapsing structures
- temporary lockdowns

It is distinct from the Burning Tsunami.

---

### THE GIGANTIC SCISSORS

Human reality:
Haircut

Meaning:
A major environmental restructuring event.

Large sections of the upper forest suddenly disappear or become drastically shorter.

This can change neighbourhood geography and transport routes.

---

### THE GREEN FIELD

Human reality:
Dan playing sport, especially football

Consequences:
- stronger shaking
- increased environmental movement
- rising moisture
- flooding
- greater risk of contact with other Heads
- increased probability of Long Foot intrusion

---

### LONG FOOTS

Human reality:
Head lice

Meaning:
Large invasive creatures from the dandruff perspective.

They are threatening because of their scale and behaviour.

Visual rule:
Their design should derive loosely from real lice anatomy but be adapted into the series’ fantasy aesthetic.

Do not turn them into generic spiders or fantasy monsters.

---

### LONG MOVING STRUCTURES

Human reality:
Dan’s fingers scratching

Meaning:
Gigantic moving structures entering the dandruff world.

Effects:
- tremors
- environmental damage
- displacement
- occasional accidental removal of citizens or structures

They should feel enormous and mysterious from dandruff scale.

---

### WET SOIL / FLOODING

Human reality:
Sweat

Visual interpretation:
Rising moisture, rain, dew, mist or flooding.

Do not depict greasy scalp.

---

### HEAD CENTRE

Central district of the dandruff civilisation.

Comparatively prosperous.

Contains:
- homes
- schools
- institutions
- commerce
- public spaces

Flake and Grandpa Snow live here.

## 3. LANGUAGE RULES
Source: `00_source/language_rules.md`

Characters normally use the terminology of their own civilisation.

Examples:

They do not usually say:
“Dan is playing football and sweating.”

They may say:
“The Boy is heading to the Green Field. Moisture levels are rising.”

They do not say:
“Dan is scratching his head.”

They may say:
“Moving Structures incoming!”

However:

Do not make every sentence full of invented terminology.

Most conversations should sound natural.

Flake and his friends should speak like ordinary young people living inside this world.

Scientists, officials and older characters may use more formal world terminology.

The audience should learn the meaning primarily through:
- visuals
- context
- cause and effect
- recurring usage

Avoid exposition-heavy dialogue.

## 4. LOCATIONS

### HEAD CENTRE
Source: `00_source/environment_brief_head_centre.md`

Design Head Centre, the central and comparatively prosperous district of the dandruff civilisation.

Head Centre is part of a fantastical micro-world inspired by hair and scalp biology, but it must NOT resemble a literal scalp.

The environment should be beautiful, strange, warm and lived-in.

### ENVIRONMENT

Enormous dark fibre-like structures rise vertically through the world, inspired by hair but visually transformed into elegant natural pillars.

They should feel ancient, immense and structurally dominant.

Around them:
- layered pale terrain
- winding paths
- bridges
- stairways
- homes
- shops
- small civic buildings
- public gathering spaces
- suspended lights
- simple transport systems
- drainage channels
- schools
- quiet residential areas

The district should feel dense but intimate.

### ARCHITECTURAL LANGUAGE

Buildings grow around or between the large vertical fibre structures.

Use:
- pale layered organic material
- fibrous structural supports
- thin thread-like bridges
- carved terraces
- membranes
- handmade fixtures
- practical mechanical devices

Do NOT create:
- literal scalp skin
- visible human pores
- greasy textures
- mushroom village
- medieval fantasy town
- steampunk city
- futuristic neon city

### SCALE

The great fibre structures should dwarf the buildings and citizens.

This constant difference in scale is part of the visual identity of the series.

### LIGHT

Warm diffuse light from far above.

Soft atmosphere.
Floating particles.
Gentle haze.
Occasional shafts of light.

The environment should feel pleasant and inhabited rather than dark or sinister.

### CAMERA

Wide cinematic establishing composition.

Strong foreground/midground/background layering.

The viewer should immediately want to explore the world.

### STYLE

Premium original animation.
Tactile materials.
Macro-cinematic sense of scale.
Warm restrained palette.
Beautiful but not overly polished.
Fantastical but internally believable.

## 5. THE HUMAN WORLD

**2026-10-20: the wool-and-cloth human world was tried and rejected.** The creator: "this Dan style is not working. the forest trees don't match this style of hair. it matched previous Dan way more." Direction: the original Dan's look, a little more refined and less generic. His hair must be smooth dark strands that match the Forest Trees. The human world is kept brief on screen.

The earlier note below is kept for history.


The creator: the head world and its characters "look good and original, but the real world aka Dan looks like pure AI slop". Dan was redesigned in wool and cloth, and "the world around him needs to match his style".

- Everything on the human side — people, buildings, streets, plants, objects — is built from wool, felt, yarn, knitting and cloth, as a handmade miniature world.
- The dandruff world is paper and fibre; the human world is wool and cloth. Each is handmade; neither looks like generic 3D animation.
- The human world is kept brief on screen (see the Part 1 opening format).
