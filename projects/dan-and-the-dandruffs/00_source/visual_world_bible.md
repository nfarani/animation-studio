# DAN & THE DANDRUFFS — VISUAL WORLD BIBLE
(Supplied by creator on 2026-10-06. Saved verbatim.)

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Hair and scalp are the hidden origin of the world, not the visible aesthetic.

The dandruff civilisation exists because of Dan’s hair and scalp, but their world should NOT look like a literal medical close-up of human skin.

The world is fantastical, beautiful, lived-in, and imaginative.

Biology provides the underlying logic.

Fantasy and civilisation provide the visible experience.

## WORLD FEEL

The environment should feel:
- warm
- strange
- tactile
- imaginative
- lived-in
- slightly mysterious
- organic
- cinematic
- visually inviting

It should NOT feel:
- disgusting
- greasy
- medical
- dermatological
- infected
- dirty
- grotesque

## BIOLOGICAL INSPIRATION

Human hair becomes:
towering fibre-like natural structures

Scalp geography becomes:
soft ridges, valleys, layered terrain, cliffs and pathways

Hair roots/follicles inspire:
deep wells, foundations, caverns, structural anchors, neighbourhood centres

Sweat becomes:
mist, rain, dew, rising moisture, flooding

Scratching becomes:
massive moving structures entering the world and causing tremors

Washing becomes:
storms, floods and environmental upheaval

Anti-dandruff shampoo becomes:
the catastrophic Burning Tsunami

Lice become:
Long Foot creatures

## IMPORTANT

These transformations must remain visually poetic.

Do not show literal:
- pores
- greasy skin
- inflamed scalp
- flakes sitting on skin
- medical scalp photography
- dermatological textures

## HUMAN ELEMENTS

The civilisation should contain familiar social structures:
- homes
- schools
- shops
- laboratories
- public squares
- transport
- parks
- neighbourhoods
- institutions
- roads/pathways
- family life

These human elements should be adapted to the world rather than copied directly from modern Earth.

## ARCHITECTURE

Architecture should feel built from materials naturally available in the world.

Use:
- fibres
- layered sheet-like material
- thread-like structural elements
- pale organic surfaces
- translucent membranes
- natural rigid fragments
- handmade mechanical systems

Avoid:
- generic medieval fantasy
- mushroom houses
- steampunk cliché
- neon cyberpunk
- modern office buildings
- Pixar fantasy town
