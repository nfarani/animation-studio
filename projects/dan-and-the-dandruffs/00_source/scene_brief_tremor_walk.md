# SCENE BRIEF — Flake walks through Head Centre; a tremor
(Supplied by creator on 2026-10-06. Saved verbatim.)

Flake walks slowly through Head Centre carrying schoolbooks.

Natural walking pace.
Subtle arm movement.
Small shifts in posture.
He occasionally looks around.
Background citizens move naturally but remain secondary.

The giant hair shafts sway almost imperceptibly.

Suddenly a distant low vibration passes through the environment.

A suspended lamp swings slightly.

Flake stops.

Looks upward.

Another stronger vibration.

A distant warning bell begins ringing.

Flake closes his eyes briefly, exhales, and says with mild annoyance:

“Oh, come on.”

Do not make him scared.
Do not exaggerate the reaction.
It should feel like an inconvenient event he has experienced before.
