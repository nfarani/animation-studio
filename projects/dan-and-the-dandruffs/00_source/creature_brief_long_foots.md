# CREATURE BRIEF — THE LONG FOOTS
(Supplied by creator on 2026-10-07. Saved verbatim.)

Create the Long Foots, the invasive monster-like creatures of the Dan & the Dandruffs world.

Long Foots are based on head lice from the dandruff perspective.

They must feel:
- large
- threatening
- invasive
- biologically plausible
- species-specific
- frightening at dandruff scale

Do NOT turn them into generic spiders, fantasy monsters, or cartoon beasts.

Their design should derive loosely from real lice anatomy, translated into the visual language of the project.

They should feel:
- unsettling
- fast
- dangerous
- memorable

Render one or more Long Foot creatures clearly in a clean presentation or dramatic character-reveal composition.
