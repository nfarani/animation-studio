(Supplied by creator on 2026-10-06. Saved verbatim.)

Design the Scalpence Centre, the main scientific and monitoring institution in Dan & the Dandruffs.

This is NOT an Inside Out-style futuristic control room.

It should feel like a scientific institution built by a fantastical microscopic civilisation using world-native materials and analogue-looking systems.

Include:
- observation towers
- physical instruments
- lenses
- charts
- seismic devices
- warning systems
- workstations
- monitoring spaces
- scientific study areas
- practical, functional architecture

The space should feel:
- intelligent
- organised
- active
- believable
- world-native
- slightly old-world but inventive

Use the established D&D world language:
organic materials, fibre-like structures, layered pale surfaces, thread-like details, tactile construction, macro-cinematic scale, warm atmospheric light.
