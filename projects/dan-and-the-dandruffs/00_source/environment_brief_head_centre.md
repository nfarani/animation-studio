# HEAD CENTRE — CANONICAL ENVIRONMENT
(Supplied by creator on 2026-10-06. Saved verbatim.)

Design Head Centre, the central and comparatively prosperous district of the dandruff civilisation.

Head Centre is part of a fantastical micro-world inspired by hair and scalp biology, but it must NOT resemble a literal scalp.

The environment should be beautiful, strange, warm and lived-in.

## ENVIRONMENT

Enormous dark fibre-like structures rise vertically through the world, inspired by hair but visually transformed into elegant natural pillars.

They should feel ancient, immense and structurally dominant.

Around them:
- layered pale terrain
- winding paths
- bridges
- stairways
- homes
- shops
- small civic buildings
- public gathering spaces
- suspended lights
- simple transport systems
- drainage channels
- schools
- quiet residential areas

The district should feel dense but intimate.

## ARCHITECTURAL LANGUAGE

Buildings grow around or between the large vertical fibre structures.

Use:
- pale layered organic material
- fibrous structural supports
- thin thread-like bridges
- carved terraces
- membranes
- handmade fixtures
- practical mechanical devices

Do NOT create:
- literal scalp skin
- visible human pores
- greasy textures
- mushroom village
- medieval fantasy town
- steampunk city
- futuristic neon city

## SCALE

The great fibre structures should dwarf the buildings and citizens.

This constant difference in scale is part of the visual identity of the series.

## LIGHT

Warm diffuse light from far above.

Soft atmosphere.
Floating particles.
Gentle haze.
Occasional shafts of light.

The environment should feel pleasant and inhabited rather than dark or sinister.

## CAMERA

Wide cinematic establishing composition.

Strong foreground/midground/background layering.

The viewer should immediately want to explore the world.

## STYLE

Premium original animation.
Tactile materials.
Macro-cinematic sense of scale.
Warm restrained palette.
Beautiful but not overly polished.
Fantastical but internally believable.
