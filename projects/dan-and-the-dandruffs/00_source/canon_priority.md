# DAN & THE DANDRUFFS — CANON PRIORITY
(Supplied by creator on 2026-10-06. Saved verbatim.)

When generating assets, scenes, prompts or scripts, follow this hierarchy:

1. WORLD_BIBLE.md
2. CHARACTER_BIBLE.md
3. STYLE_BIBLE.md
4. Approved master reference images
5. Episode script
6. Individual shot prompt

Lower-level material may add detail but must not contradict higher-level canon.

If an established term appears in a script:
consult the World Bible before interpreting it.

Never reinterpret an established world term.

Example:
Burning Tsunami = anti-dandruff shampoo.

Do not generate:
fire
lava
literal flames
unless explicitly required by canon.
