# CHARACTER BRIEF — FLAKE
(Supplied by creator on 2026-10-06. Saved verbatim.)

Create an original animated character design for a tiny sentient dandruff creature named Flake.

He lives in a microscopic civilisation inside a human scalp, but the design must NOT look like Pixar, Inside Out, Minions, DreamWorks, or a generic 3D mascot.

Flake should feel tactile, unusual and memorable.

DESIGN:
- tiny pale creature
- body shape roughly circular but slightly imperfect
- made from soft compressed fibrous material, somewhere between lint, paper fibre, snowflake texture and soft organic fluff
- subtle frayed edges
- not smooth plastic
- not glossy
- not perfectly symmetrical
- large expressive eyes, but restrained proportions, not oversized cartoon eyes
- small limbs
- youthful, curious, slightly rebellious personality
- brave but not heroic-looking
- ordinary kid energy
- visually appealing without looking cute-for-the-sake-of-cute

FACE:
- expressive eyebrows
- intelligent eyes
- subtle asymmetry
- small mouth
- expressions should feel natural rather than exaggerated

CLOTHING:
- simple everyday clothing adapted to his world
- small jacket or shirt
- practical trousers
- tiny worn shoes
- clothing should look handmade from materials available in the dandruff civilisation
- muted natural tones, not bright toy colours

VISUAL STYLE:
- premium cinematic animation
- tactile materials
- macro photography feeling
- shallow depth of field
- subtle imperfections
- soft directional light
- natural material response
- sophisticated children’s animation
- original visual language
- warm but slightly strange
- believable as a species

BACKGROUND:
simple neutral studio-style environment with soft light, so the character design is easy to evaluate.

Generate multiple distinct design directions while preserving the same core character concept.

Avoid:
Pixar-style proportions
Inside Out-style character design
plastic skin
glossy surfaces
giant eyes
overly cute mascot design
bright primary colours
generic fantasy creature design
human child proportions
