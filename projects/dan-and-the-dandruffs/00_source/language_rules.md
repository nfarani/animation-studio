# DAN & THE DANDRUFFS — LANGUAGE RULES
(Supplied by creator on 2026-10-06. Saved verbatim.)

Characters normally use the terminology of their own civilisation.

Examples:

They do not usually say:
“Dan is playing football and sweating.”

They may say:
“The Boy is heading to the Green Field. Moisture levels are rising.”

They do not say:
“Dan is scratching his head.”

They may say:
“Moving Structures incoming!”

However:

Do not make every sentence full of invented terminology.

Most conversations should sound natural.

Flake and his friends should speak like ordinary young people living inside this world.

Scientists, officials and older characters may use more formal world terminology.

The audience should learn the meaning primarily through:
- visuals
- context
- cause and effect
- recurring usage

Avoid exposition-heavy dialogue.
