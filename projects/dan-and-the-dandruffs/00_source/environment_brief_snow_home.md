# GRANDPA SNOW'S HOME — ENVIRONMENT BRIEF
(Supplied by creator on 2026-10-07. Saved verbatim.)

Design Grandpa Snow’s home, where Flake lives with him, in the Dan & the Dandruffs world.

This is a modest but memorable 3-storey home in Head Centre, built down into and around the root of one of the great fibre-like “Forest Trees.”

The house should feel:
- warm
- old
- lived-in
- slightly worn
- safe
- intimate
- full of history

IMPORTANT:
This is one of the key recurring interiors of the series.
It must feel canonical and distinctive.

LAYOUT / IDENTITY
- a narrow but cosy multi-level home
- built vertically within the layered terrain around a giant root structure
- a cylindrical or curved staircase connecting levels
- warm domestic scale
- world-native architecture and materials
- signs that an elderly legend and his grandson live here

MATERIALS / WORLD LOGIC
Use the Dan & the Dandruffs visual world language:
- layered pale organic surfaces
- fibrous structural details
- thread-like elements
- handmade fixtures
- soft practical lighting
- tactile matte materials
- no literal scalp grossness
- no modern human suburban realism

INTERIOR CHARACTER
The house should include visual hints of:
- Grandpa Snow’s past as a survivor/hero of the 4th Burning Tsunami
- Flake’s youth and ordinary daily life
- modest family life
- history, memory and resilience

Possible details:
- books
- old tools
- framed keepsakes
- practical storage
- woven or layered furnishings
- handmade domestic objects
- small warning devices or weather instruments
- signs of age and repair

MOOD
The home should feel comforting and slightly eccentric, with emotional weight but no melodrama.

Create a canonical hero interior image showing the home clearly as a recurring set.
