## FLAKE
**Status:** design CREATOR-APPROVED 2026-10-06 (the creator chose concept B as the base, then called the refined result "the approved Flake design").

### Canon facts (*creator*)
- A tiny sentient dandruff creature. Lives in Head Centre with Grandpa Snow.
- Youthful, curious, clever, slightly rebellious, warm and adventurous. Notices things, questions authority and occasionally gets himself into trouble.
- Not tired, elderly or gloomy. Brave but not heroic-looking. Ordinary kid energy.
- A member of an original species, not a tiny human child transformed into dandruff. Immediately recognisable in silhouette.
- Speaks like an ordinary young person living inside this world (see WORLD_BIBLE, Language rules).

### Design rules (*creator*, from the refinement brief)
- Irregular flattened flake-like body; torn layered edge; visible fibres.
- The face is integrated into the body, not placed on a separate human-like head.
- Thin simple limbs emerging naturally from the flake body; species-specific, not miniature human arms and legs.
- Body made of compressed layers of pale fibrous flake material: visible fine fibres, subtle layering, torn irregular edges, matte surface, slightly soft but not fluffy, organic and tactile.
- Eyes expressive but restrained; never oversized or conventionally cute. Strong expressive eyebrows, subtle facial asymmetry, a small mouth with a mischievous quality.
- Expressions restrained and natural. The silhouette must not deform heavily with emotion.
- Clothing: a small practical jacket or overshirt, a simple lower-body garment suited to his anatomy, worn small shoes; muted natural colours; fabrics native to his world; clothes must not overpower the flake silhouette. A normal young person of his civilisation, not a fantasy adventurer.
- Avoid: smooth human skin, a separate human face, ceramic surfaces, translucent shell texture, fuzzy mascot fur, plastic CGI, doll-like faces, giant eyes, glossy CGI, stop-motion toy aesthetics.

### As approved (*from the approved image*)
- Body: a flat, round, slightly lopsided disc, a few layers thick; cream-white with faint creases and small specks; the torn rim shows separate layers. Seen from the side he is a thin stack of sheets.
- Face: on the front of the disc. Round, dark, matte eyes in soft raised lids; tufted fibre eyebrows, one higher than the other; a small lopsided closed-mouth smile; no nose.
- Limbs: thin twisted pale cord; small simple hands.
- Clothing: a faded ochre-clay woven overshirt with a collar, rolled cuffs, visible hand stitching, one pale mended patch on the front and one toggle; grey-moss green shorts; small scuffed stitched shoes with ties.
- Habitual pose: one hand hooked into the front of his overshirt.

## GRANDPA SNOW
**Status:** design CREATOR-APPROVED 2026-10-06 ("ok grandpa approved", on hero round 2).

### Canon facts (*creator*)
- Flake's grandfather. Lives in Head Centre with Flake. Deeply loved by Flake.
- Elderly. Legendary survivor of the 4th Burning Tsunami.
- Eccentric, increasingly senile, funny, proud, brave.
- Warm, memorable and slightly comedic without becoming silly.

### Design rules (*creator*)
- The established species language: irregular flattened flake-like body, integrated face, fibrous layered material, thin limbs, matte tactile surface.
- Visibly an older version of the species: softened, worn, weathered edges; looser fibre structure; slightly stooped posture.
- Expressive elderly face; kind eyes; hints of former toughness and dignity.
- Simple old-fashioned clothing native to the dandruff world.

### As approved (*from the approved image*)
- Body: a flat disc like Flake's but larger and broader, greyer ivory, deeply creased, with a small notch missing from the upper edge. No nose.

- Clothing: a darned slate-blue woven waistcoat with two patches over a rust shirt; a small gold-coloured medal on a pale ribbon on the chest; loose clay-brown trousers; worn soft shoes.
- Carries a twisted-cord walking stick with a crook handle; tends to keep his other arm behind his back.
- The moustache, stick and medal began as the director's additions and became canon when the creator approved this image.
- No mark on him may read as a burn: the Burning Tsunami is a liquid event (WORLD_BIBLE).

## PALE
**Status:** design CREATOR-APPROVED 2026-10-06 ("ok approved", on hero round 4).

### Canon facts (*creator*)
- Flake's best friend. Close to Flake. Young.
- Slightly smarter and more academic than Flake. Practical. Observant. Caring.
- Self-conscious about her uneven shape. Anxious about danger.

### Design rules (*creator*)
- The established species language: irregular flattened flake-like body, integrated face, fibrous layered material, thin simple limbs, matte tactile surface. Recognisably the same species as Flake, but not a recoloured copy.
- A slightly more asymmetrical or uneven flake silhouette than Flake.
- Intelligent alert eyes; thoughtful expression; slightly reserved posture.
- Simple handmade clothing with a modest academic feel; muted natural colours.
- Young, believable, emotionally readable, species-consistent.

### As approved (*from the approved image*)
- Body: built like Flake (the flake is the whole body; short cord limbs; same squat scale) but strongly uneven: a corner juts out on one side at eye height and the upper edge rises to a point on the other. Cool, pale, faintly bluish-white fibre with grey flecks. A tuft of fibre tied with thread at the top. No nose.
- Face: dark matte almond-shaped eyes with darker rims along the upper lids; fine fibre eyebrows, relaxed; a small warm lopsided smile.
- Clothing: dusty sage woven overshirt with a small collar; pleated slate lower garment; tidy grey stitched shoes.
- Carries: a small satchel on a cross-body strap, an observation lens on a cord at her collar, a thread-bound notebook held to her front.
- The tuft, satchel, lens and notebook began as the director's additions and became canon when the creator approved this image.
- Expression note (*creator*): an earlier version was rejected because "she looks angry". Lowered, drawn-together brows are wrong for her; show thought or worry by raising the inner ends of the brows.

## DAN — "THE BOY"
**Status:** the original design, subtly refined, is the current design (2026-10-20): `assets/CHAR_DAN_MASTER/hero_v5.png`. Same boy, age, proportions and outfit as the original; matte hand-painted finish, calmer eyes, a smaller lopsided smile. It is what Part 1 now shows.

Rejected along the way: wool and cloth ("the forest trees don't match this style of hair"); a pushed redesign ("he looks too old now"). **Rules from the creator:** keep the regular Dan, refined, less generic; don't overcomplicate; his hair stays smooth dark strands that match the Forest Trees.

### Canon facts (*creator*)
- A human boy; the dandruffs call him The Boy (WORLD_BIBLE section 2). His daily life shapes their world. Not worshipped; most dandruffs have never seen him as a complete person.
- An ordinary kid: school age, energetic, playful, believable, relatable and normal.
- Goes to school, plays football, sometimes misbehaves.
- From the dandruff perspective he is the huge unknowable external being.

### Design rules (*creator*)
- Human, not part of the dandruff species.
- A generic 3D-animated look is not acceptable (creator, 2026-10-07).
- ~~Made of wool and cloth~~ — tried and rejected 2026-10-20.
- Slightly messy hair; casual everyday clothes; friendly but not overly posed.

### As approved (*from the chosen direction and the hero image built from it*)
- A soft, hand-made figure: needle-felted wool face and hands with a fuzzy surface; a small felted bump of a nose; two tiny dark bead eyes; a short stitched mouth; pink wool blush on the cheeks; mitten-like hands.
- Hair: very dark brown, almost black, thick wool yarn and roving; wild, messy and tufty. Dark, to agree with the Forest Trees.
- Clothes: a chunky hand-knitted grey zip hoodie with a tiny metal zip, worn open; a faded ochre woven T-shirt; dark teal woven shorts; odd knitted socks; felted trainers with stitched stripes. A felted football.
- About nine or ten. Small friendly closed-mouth smile.

### Continuity rule (director, follows from WORLD_BIBLE)
His hair is the Sacred Forest. Its colour and character should agree with the Forest Trees as designed: dark and strong.

## LONG FOOTS
**Status:** design CREATOR-APPROVED 2026-10-07 ("yes that will do", on reveal round 3, as generated).

### Canon facts (*creator*)
- The invasive monster-like creatures of the world; head lice from the dandruff perspective (WORLD_BIBLE section 2).
- Large, threatening, invasive, biologically plausible, species-specific, frightening at dandruff scale.
- Unsettling, fast, dangerous, memorable.

### Design rules (*creator*)
- Derived loosely from real lice anatomy, translated into the visual language of the project.
- Not generic spiders, fantasy monsters or cartoon beasts.
- Must match the characters and the world, and must not look disgusting: the first two versions, rendered as insect flesh, were rejected on those grounds (creator, 2026-10-07).

### As approved (*from the approved image*)
- A louse's silhouette built from the world's handmade materials, not flesh.
- Body: a long, low row of overlapping plates of dark slate-grey handmade sheet with torn edges, like heavy shingles, with stiff straw-like bristles between them.
- Legs: six, all attached just behind the head; thick twisted rope lashed with lighter cord at the joints.
- Claws: one large hooked claw per leg, a pale bone-coloured fragment lashed on, clamping round a Forest Tree.
- Head: a blunt wedge folded from dark sheet with a heavy brow; a flat, pale, half-lidded disc eye on the side; two short knotted-cord antennae; a short pale beak.
- Scale: vast. A bridge lantern is about the size of one claw.
- Dark and cold against the warm world. They travel by clamping from one Forest Tree to the next.
- These details began as the director's interpretation and became canon when the creator approved this image.
