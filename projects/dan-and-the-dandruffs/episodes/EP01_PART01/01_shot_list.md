## Coverage plan for General Arrow's speech
Two long takes carry all of his orders (10 and 11), so his voice and mouth stay continuous inside each. The cutaways (10a–10c, 11a) are laid over those takes while his sound runs on. If a mouth is wrong in either take, the cutaways and the wide can cover it.

## What is deliberately held back
- The Long Foots are named and never shown.
- "Contaminated Heads" and "wet-soil warning" are said and not explained.
- The observer's lens shows only the bright sky.

## Chained shots
3 → 4 → 5 are built between shared keyframes so the move from Dan's hair into the world is one continuous descent.
